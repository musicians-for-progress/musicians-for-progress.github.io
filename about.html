About Musicians for Progress

Music is a language in itself, and in it we find our acts of resistance.

Who We Are

Musicians for Progress is a New York City-based nonprofit collective of classical and contemporary musicians. We organize benefit concerts and outreach programs that direct funds, visibility, and volunteer energy toward organizations doing frontline work in our communities — from immigrant and refugee services to housing and shelter support.

We believe music has always been part of movements for change, and that musicians have a role to play beyond the concert hall.

Our Focus

We partner with grassroots and community organizations to design concerts and events around a specific cause — from immigrant rights to housing insecurity — and direct the proceeds and awareness generated directly to that partner.

Our Beliefs

Musicians for Progress is an inclusive organization. We stand in support of LGBTQ+ rights, reproductive rights, and immigrant communities, and we welcome musicians and audiences of every background to join us.
