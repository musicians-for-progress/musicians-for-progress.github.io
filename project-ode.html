Project O.D.E

Outreach, Development, Engagement.

Project O.D.E. (Outreach, Development, Engagement) is a 2026-2027 outreach initiative focused on bringing music into underserved spaces while getting emerging musicians out of the practice room and into the world.

Through this project, we are building long-term community partnerships that are designed to benefit the hosting organization and the services they provide by sending chamber music groups into their spaces. We believe strongly in chamber music and its role as a form of collaboration and empowerment, as well as music as a form of wellness and source of joy.
